🍽️ Food Waste

What counts as food waste?

  • Leftover cooked meals
  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Expired food
  • Coffee grounds, tea bags
  • Bread, grains, dairy (if not recyclable)

This waste is ideal for biogas production via anaerobic digestion.

📦 Plastic Waste

Plastic waste includes:

  • Plastic bags, bottles, containers
  • Food packaging
  • Non-recyclable mixed plastics

Note: Not suitable for biogas. Best recycled or converted via waste-to-energy (thermal) processes.

📄 Organic Waste

Organic waste includes:

  • Newspapers, cardboard, paper
  • Garden trimmings, leaves
  • Wood chips, sawdust
  • Untreated natural fibers

Great for composting and biogas. Avoid glossy or plastic-coated paper.
